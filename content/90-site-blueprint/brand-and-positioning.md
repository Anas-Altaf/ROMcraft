# Brand: ROMcraft Fieldnotes

> **Scope:** Naming, tagline, visual personality and content positioning.  
> **Evidence:** planned. See [Evidence policy](../00-start-here/evidence-and-accuracy.md).

## Name

**ROMcraft Fieldnotes** — a distinctive, descriptive identity that combines the practical craft of Android ROM work with rigorous observations from real experiments.

- Repository slug: `romcraft-fieldnotes`.
- Website short label: **ROMcraft**.
- Tagline: **Document the boot. Verify the build. Own the device.**
- Editorial tone: calm, precise, candid about mistakes; never sensationalize 'unbrick guarantees'.

## Brand extensions

- **Device Labs**: complete field studies.
- **Field Guides**: generic how-tos.
- **Recovery Desk**: troubleshooting.
- **Trust Notes**: security audit and provenance.
- **ROM Atlas**: software comparison and status.
- **Build Journal**: source builds and kernel work.

## Visual direction

Deep navy/ink surfaces, muted slate and restrained electric cyan/teal accent. Headlines use a modern grotesk/system font; command/code use an accessible monospaced face. Rounded panels should serve real structure, not excessive card clutter.

## Avoid

Samsung and LineageOS trademarks used as if the hub were officially affiliated; fake performance numbers, undisclosed paid content, made-up downloads or blurred official/unofficial status.
