# Samsung Galaxy A10 SM-A105F — field study

> **Scope:** End-to-end index for this exact model's 2026 experiments.  
> **Evidence:** field tested. See [Evidence policy](../../00-start-here/evidence-and-accuracy.md).

## Outcome

Device launched with official Samsung Android 11. Research established functioning Magisk root, TWRP, LineageOS 23.2 unofficial Android 16 and finally /e/OS 4.1.1 unofficial Android 14. All basic hardware functions were observed working on /e/OS. Root intentionally not installed on the final system.
