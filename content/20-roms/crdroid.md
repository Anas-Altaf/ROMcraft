# crDroid on the Galaxy A10

> **Scope:** Candidate assessment: customization versus outdated A10 maintenance.  
> **Evidence:** community report. See [Evidence policy](../00-start-here/evidence-and-accuracy.md).

## Candidate

Research identified crDroid **10.10** based on Android 14 for codename `a10`, with device-specific archive `crDroidAndroid-14.0-20241117-a10-v10.10.zip`. The A10 device line was identified as unsupported/outdated in prior research and **was not installed in our field session**.

## Trade-offs

crDroid usually emphasizes customization and Pixel/AOSP-adjacent UI. It may appeal to users who want fine-grained visual control, but an older unsupported device build can lag security fixes. No A10 head-to-head performance benchmark was collected; avoid claiming it definitely outperforms /e/OS or LineageOS.

## Decision in the session

Not selected. The user tested LineageOS and later preferred /e/OS privacy/launcher experience.
